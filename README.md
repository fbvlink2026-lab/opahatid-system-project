## 🎯 PROJECT OVERVIEW

**PAHATID SYSTEM** is a comprehensive, web-based platform designed specifically for **Habal-Habal (Motorcycle Taxi)** transport services in the Philippines. It connects **Commuters**, **Drivers**, and **Admin/Dispatchers** in one unified ecosystem — ensuring fair fares, transparent earnings, efficient dispatch, and secure transactions.

### Mission
- ✅ Accurate fare & income computation for drivers
- ✅ Safe, reliable booking & tracking for commuters
- ✅ Systematic management & accountability for admins
- ✅ Promote professionalism in Habal-Habal transport
